# Pipeline failure modes — why solve → export → sim is fragile

Companion to `EVALUATION.md`. Every entry is a place where **two stages assume a contract that
nothing checks**. Measured numbers are from the 2026-07-26 session unless marked *(known)* or
*(inferred)*. `[F]` = fixed that session, `[O]` = open.

**The one-sentence diagnosis.** The planner, the motion planner and the sim each carry their own
model of geometry, tolerance and force, and the pipeline hands data between them with no
cross-stage assertion. Almost every failure below is an *undetected disagreement*, not a bug in
any single stage — which is why they surface as "the sim missed a seat by 32mm" a hundred steps
downstream instead of as an error at the boundary.

## 4. Force contract *(user's "forces inconsistent / not in motion planning")*

**The motion planner does not model force at all.** The wrench LP runs in the discrete search; the
sim re-derives loads independently; `--force-limits` is an execution-side retiming afterthought.
Nothing reconciles the three.

## To make this reliable, in order

1. **Close the round-trip (1.1, 1.2).** Until a plan+path can be regenerated bit-for-bit, no
   measurement is trustworthy and every A/B is confounded. This is the cheapest and highest-value
   fix, and it is what wasted the most time in practice.
2. **Fail at the boundary, not downstream (3.2, 7.3, 1.3, 3.4).** Refuse to weld beyond
   `SEAT_POS_TOL`; abort on the first missed seat rather than cascading; error on no-op invocations;
   make the unvalidated funnel fallback an error.
3. **One geometry, one tolerance (2.1, 2.2, 3.1).** Pick the collision model, make
   `--pre-insertion` the default, derive the three tolerances from one number and assert them.
4. **Put force in the motion planner (4.1, 4.7, 4.2).** Today it is a discrete-search input and an
   execution-side retiming, with nothing in between — so the exported path is the one artefact no
   force model ever sees.
5. **Fix the mode semantics (5.1, 5.2).** Both are the same `BIG TODO(mp)`: a held/braced gripper
   is a relative-pose constraint, not a joint goal.
6. **Add outcome tests (7.1).** One test per scene asserting the known sim result would have caught
   the `NameError`, the `--mass-scale` `StopIteration`, and the compiler-tag bug immediately.
